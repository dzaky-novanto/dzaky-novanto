## 👋 About Me

I'm **Dzaky**, a student of **IT Network Engineering (TKJ)** with a strong interest in how computer networks and systems actually work under the hood.

Most of my free time goes into tinkering — spinning up VMs, breaking things on purpose, and figuring out how to fix them again. I'm not chasing one single path; I like bouncing between **networking, Linux administration, cybersecurity basics, and web development**, and building small projects along the way to actually test what I've learned instead of just reading about it.

Still very much a work in progress — but that's the point.
